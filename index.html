Server Rules

Simple rules. Follow them and everyone has a good time.

01

Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or toxic behavior. Treat people like humans.

02

No spam or self-promo

Keep chats clean. No mass pinging, advertising other servers, or flooding channels.

03

Keep it appropriate

NSFW content stays out of public channels. Follow Discord ToS at all times.

04

Listen to staff

Moderators and admins are here to keep the server healthy. Their word is final.

05

Have fun

This is a gaming community. Play games, make friends, and enjoy the ride.
